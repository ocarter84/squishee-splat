# Squishee Splat

A cartoon first-person shooter. The squishees broke out of the jelly jar and are bouncing all over the candy yard. Pop them with your goo blaster before they bounce you flat.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play. It loads Three.js r128 from cdnjs, so it needs an internet connection.

## Controls

- WASD or arrow keys: move
- Mouse: aim (click the game to lock the mouse)
- Click: blast goo (hold for rapid fire)
- Space: hop
- M: sound on or off
- Esc: pause

On phones and tablets, use the on-screen stick, Blast and Hop buttons.

## How it plays

- Small squishees are fast, medium ones take 3 hits, big ones take 7 and split in two.
- Each wave brings more squishees. Clearing a wave refills 25 of your squish meter.
- Pop squishees quickly to build a combo, up to x5 points.
